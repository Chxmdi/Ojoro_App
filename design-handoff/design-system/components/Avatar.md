# Avatar

A person, drawn as the O: a disc with photo or initials, or a dashed empty slot.

**Properties** `name` (required for the label), `src`, `size` (`sm` 28 · `md` 40 · `lg` 72), `empty` (an open spot in a plan).
**Rules** initials sit on `tint` in `on-tint`; photos are candid, never stock. Avatars are the only always-round thing besides pills and stamps.
**Accessibility** `role=img` labelled with the name; empty reads "Open spot".
