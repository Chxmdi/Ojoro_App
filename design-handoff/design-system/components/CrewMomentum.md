# CrewMomentum

A crew's header: name, members, eight weeks of rhythm and the next thing on.

**Properties** `crew`, `memberCount`, `members`, `weeks`, `streak`, `next`.
**Rules** rhythm is shown as plain bars, never XP; the streak is a count in words. The I'M DOWN here joins the next plan directly.
**Analytics** `crew_viewed`, `crew_next_joined`.
