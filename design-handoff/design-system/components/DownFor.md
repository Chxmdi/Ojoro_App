# DownFor

The DOWN FOR prompt: an editorial question ("FOOTBALL TONIGHT?") and the I'M DOWN commit.

**Properties** `activity`, `when`, `where`, `downCount`, `downs`, `committed`, `onDown`.
**Composition** editorial block — no card. Display question, body line, cluster, full-width commit button.
**Motion** Commit on press; your avatar Gathers into the cluster.
**Haptic** Light impact on I'M DOWN. **Analytics** `downfor_viewed`, `im_down_tapped`.
**Accessibility** "Football tonight. 11 people down." then the button.
