# LiveIndicator

The LIVE pill: oxblood, the word LIVE in white, a pulsing white dot and a `mark-ring`.

**Rules** oxblood and black are 1.09:1 — the ring is mandatory, never remove it. Only one LIVE indicator per screen.
**Motion** Live — dot pulses on `dur-live`; static under reduced motion.
**Accessibility** `role=status`; the word LIVE is the indicator, not the dot.
