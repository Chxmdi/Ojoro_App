# PlaceMiniCard

A place as a strip: the O with a pin, name, what it's for, and how many plans are on there tonight.

**Properties** `name`, `kind`, `surface`, `distance`, `lights` (floodlit until), `plansTonight`, `href`.
**Rules** places are real-world anchors, never decorated — no map thumbnails in strips (the map has its own screen).
**Accessibility** one link, `size-touch` tall; the label includes the count.
