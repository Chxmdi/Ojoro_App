# PredictionBar

WHO TAKES IT? — the crowd's split prediction for a Challenge Event.

**Properties** `question`, `a`/`b` `{name, pct}`, `picked` (the viewer's side).
**Rules** each side is named and numbered at its end; segments are `signal` vs `tint` with a gap — never two hues told apart by colour.
**Analytics** `prediction_made{side}`.
**Accessibility** one label: "Who takes it? Chimdi 48 percent, Marcus 52 percent".
