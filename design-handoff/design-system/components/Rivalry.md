# Rivalry

Head-to-head history between two people: the record in huge numerals and the form line.

**Properties** `a`, `b`, `record` (`{a, b, draws}`), `history` (`'a' | 'b' | 'd'`, oldest first), `next`.
**Rules** the live situation. Form cells carry the winner's initial (or = for a draw) — filled for one side, outlined for the other.
**Accessibility** the record is read as a sentence; each form cell is labelled with the winner's name.
