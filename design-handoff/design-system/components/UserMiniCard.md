# UserMiniCard

A compact physical identity: who they are, what they play and how much — shown on tap of an avatar.

**Properties** `name`, `handle`, `area`, `verified`, `sports`, `stats` (counts, never scores or levels), `following`, `onFollow`, `onCallOut`.
**Rules** contained (`surface-raised`) because it floats over content. CALL OUT is always secondary to FOLLOW.
**Analytics** `profile_peeked`, `follow_tapped`, `callout_started`.
